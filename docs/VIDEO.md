# Demo video script (about 4:30, six scenes)

Everything on screen is the Codegeist demo site. One work item, MOBL-53, carries the story: Blocked in scene 2, Clear after the fix in scene 3. How it is filmed, take by take, with the site state each take needs: `docs/SHOOTING-SCRIPT.md`. Captions only, no voice-over (owner's choice, 2026-11-27). The labels in the Screen column are the ones the panel shows today (`src/frontend/index.tsx`, `src/frontend/decisions.tsx`).

One story, one set of decisions. The video uses the project's sample decisions ("Load sample decisions (demo)") and nothing else, so a judge who loads them can repeat every step. The two that matter: "Work ships by the end of 2026" (a date limit of 2026-12-31) and "Stop using the legacy billing component" (the component `legacy-billing` must not be added). The read-backs are the ones in `docs/DEVPOST.md` and the README, word for word. Refused: "Work ships by the end of 2026: I will ship on 15 January 2027. Stop using the legacy billing component: understood." Accepted: "Work ships by the end of 2026: I will ship by 15 December 2026. Stop using the legacy billing component: understood."

Why 4:30 and not 3:00 (owner's choice, 2026-11-27): the rules ask the video to show the specifics of each bonus prize, and three apply (`docs/JUDGING.md`): Best Agents (Rovo), Best app with Atlassian MCP, and Best apps with Teamwork Graph integration. The extra time goes to one shot per prize and to a slower pace, not to more features. The main story (Blocked, Clear, the validator) is over by 2:20, so a judge who stops there has seen it. The cut stays at 4:45 or less.

## Before filming

- Two accounts, each in its own Chrome profile with Claude in Chrome, so that Claude can drive both (owner's choice, 2026-11-27): the sender (the owner, a project administrator) and the receiver (Meo Vat IT). Only the receiver can give the read-back (the panel checks it on the server), so scenes 3 and 5 need both.
- Rehearse the whole path on **production**, the build a judge installs. The draft of 2026-11-27 is that rehearsal. On 2026-11-15 the validator was seen on production refusing a blocked item and letting a Clear handoff through (`docs/DEVPOST.md`). What the draft showed goes in `docs/PROGRESS.md`.
- The staging panel ("Handoff Guard STG") must not be installed on the site, so the filmed panel is the only one.
- Click "Load sample decisions (demo)" on the project so decisions exist. Film with an account that is a project administrator: the button, Confirm, Supersede and "Add a decision" are hidden from anyone else. On a project with no decision the panel puts this button first.
- The practice read-back box shows beside the next step when the viewer is about to prepare a handoff (or cannot prepare one), with "Fill an example"; otherwise it is under "Show everything". The receiver sees the real read-back form instead.
- Blocked case (MOBL-53): a description, the due date 2027-01-15, the component `legacy-billing` (created in the project on 2026-11-27), and a prepared handoff to the receiver. The binding decisions are the two sample decisions above. The exact start state and the reset are in `docs/SHOOTING-SCRIPT.md`.
- Clear case (MOBL-53 after the fix in scene 3): the item meets both decisions (a due date of 2026-12-15), the receiver has given a read-back after the last change to the item, and the item is assigned to the receiver (or the sender, or no one). The validator refuses an item assigned to a third person even when the handoff is Clear.
- Rovo must be enabled on the site for scene 5 (c) and (d). The development agent has the same name: pick the production one, or open the chat from the production panel. Start a new chat for each try. Do not depend on Rovo for the main story: a judge's site may not have it.
- A Jira admin must have added the Handoff Guard validator to a workflow transition for scene 4.
- For scene 6 (a): a site admin has switched on "Expose tools to MCP-compatible apps" for this app on the filming site (Atlassian Administration, Apps, Sites, the site, Connected apps, Handoff Guard, Tools), the `handoff-guard` server is connected in Claude Code as an account that can open MOBL-53, and the call has answered before (2026-11-25, 2026-11-27). Judges' sites have this switch off, which is why the shot shows it working. No token or account email on screen.
- For scene 6 (b): the bounce is made on the day by take 10 of `docs/SHOOTING-SCRIPT.md`, within 7 days of the accepted read-back.
- For scene 5 (b): the panel shows "People connected (Teamwork Graph)" with a result. Over MCP on 2026-11-27, `gather-handoff-context` answered "Teamwork Graph could not be read just now", so the panel is checked before filming; if it gives the same, the cut is left out.

Rules for the cut: no real customer data, only the demo site. No measurement figure is shown on screen: a percentage in a video is read as accuracy whatever the narration says (council review, 2026-11-14). The figures stay in the README, called "agreement with labels", with the samples stated as synthetic and the labels as model-written.

Out of the video, in the product and the docs only: Leaving the project, the I-PASS labels, Activity, counts, export and delete, adding a decision by hand, Replay. Scene 3 needs two accounts: it is the only place a judge sees Clear.
