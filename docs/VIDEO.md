# Demo video script (about 3 minutes, six scenes)

Everything on screen is the Codegeist demo site `one-atlas-hxpc.atlassian.net`. Work items: MOBL-53 (blocked case) and TD-1 or MOBL-58 (clear case). Captions or voice-over in English. The labels in the Screen column are the ones the panel shows today (`src/frontend/index.tsx`, `src/frontend/decisions.tsx`).

One story, one set of decisions. The video uses the project's sample decisions ("Load sample decisions (demo)") and nothing else, so a judge who loads them can repeat every step. The two that matter: "Work ships by the end of 2026" (a date limit of 2026-12-31) and "Stop using the legacy billing component" (the component `legacy-billing` must not be added). The refused read-back is always the same one: "I will ship on 15 January 2027." The accepted one is always: "I will ship by 15 December 2026 and I will not add the legacy billing component."

The same review asked that the video say who the app is for and what a bad handoff costs; that is the caption in scene 1. No figure of our own is shown, only the cited study.

## Before filming

- Two accounts: a sender and a receiver. Only the receiver can give the read-back (the panel checks it on the server), so scenes 3 and 5 need both. Use a second browser profile or window. The second account (Meo Vat IT) has been used for this on development (see `docs/PROGRESS.md`); on production it must be a member of the project and prepared as the receiver.
- Rehearse the whole path on **production**, the build a judge installs: sample decisions loaded, a handoff to the second account taken to Clear, the validator added to a transition, one blocked item refused and the Clear item let through. On 2026-11-15 this was observed on development only (Clear verdict yes, validator pass no). Write what happened in `docs/PROGRESS.md` before filming.
- The staging panel ("Handoff Guard STG") must not be installed on the site, so the filmed panel is the only one.
- Click "Load sample decisions (demo)" on the project so decisions exist. Film with an account that is a project administrator: the button, Confirm, Supersede and "Add a decision" are hidden from anyone else. On a project with no decision the panel puts this button first.
- The practice read-back box shows whenever a decision binds the item and the viewer is not the receiver, with "Fill an example". The receiver sees the real read-back form instead.
- Blocked case (suggested: MOBL-53): a description, a due date later than 2026-12-31 or a missing due date, the component `legacy-billing`, and a prepared handoff to the receiver. The binding decisions are the two sample decisions above.
- Clear case (suggested: TD-1 or MOBL-58): the item meets both decisions (a due date of 2026-12-15), the receiver has given a read-back after the last change to the item, and the item is assigned to the receiver (or the sender, or no one). The validator refuses an item assigned to a third person even when the handoff is Clear.
- Rovo must be enabled on the site for scene 5, and the item for "Find decisions with Rovo" needs a decision written in its description or comments. Do not depend on Rovo for the main story: a judge's site may not have it.
- A Jira admin must have added the Handoff Guard validator to a workflow transition for scene 4.

Rules for the cut: no real customer data, only the demo site. No measurement figure is shown on screen: a percentage in a video is read as accuracy whatever the narration says (council review, 2026-11-14). The figures stay in the README, called "agreement with labels", with the samples stated as synthetic and the labels as model-written.

Bounce autopsy can take the third cut of scene 5 if real bounced handoffs exist on the demo site: make them by hand (reach Clear with two accounts, then reassign back to the sender), and say in the narration that this history was made for the demo. Without them, leave it out.

Out of the video, in the product and the docs only: Leaving the project, the I-PASS labels, Activity, counts, export and delete, adding a decision by hand, Replay. Scene 3 needs two accounts: it is the only place a judge sees Clear.
