# Demo video script (about 3 minutes, six scenes)

Everything on screen is the Codegeist demo site. Work items: MOBL-53 (blocked case) and TD-1 or MOBL-58 (clear case). Captions or voice-over in English. The labels in the Screen column are the ones the panel shows today (`src/frontend/index.tsx`, `src/frontend/decisions.tsx`).

One story, one set of decisions. The video uses the project's sample decisions ("Load sample decisions (demo)") and nothing else, so a judge who loads them can repeat every step. The two that matter: "Work ships by the end of 2026" (a date limit of 2026-12-31) and "Stop using the legacy billing component" (the component `legacy-billing` must not be added). The read-backs are the ones in `docs/DEVPOST.md` and the README, word for word. Refused: "Work ships by the end of 2026: I will ship on 15 January 2027. Stop using the legacy billing component: understood." Accepted: "Work ships by the end of 2026: I will ship by 15 December 2026. Stop using the legacy billing component: understood."

## Before filming

- Two accounts: a sender and a receiver. Only the receiver can give the read-back (the panel checks it on the server), so scenes 3 and 5 need both. Use a second browser profile or window. The second account (Meo Vat IT) has been used for this on development (see `docs/PROGRESS.md`); on production it must be a member of the project and prepared as the receiver.
- Rehearse the whole path on **production**, the build a judge installs: sample decisions loaded, a handoff to the second account taken to Clear, the validator added to a transition, one blocked item refused and the Clear item let through. On 2026-11-15 the validator was seen on production refusing a blocked item and letting a Clear handoff through (`docs/DEVPOST.md`). Repeat it on the final build and write what happened in `docs/PROGRESS.md` before filming.
- The staging panel ("Handoff Guard STG") must not be installed on the site, so the filmed panel is the only one.
- Click "Load sample decisions (demo)" on the project so decisions exist. Film with an account that is a project administrator: the button, Confirm, Supersede and "Add a decision" are hidden from anyone else. On a project with no decision the panel puts this button first.
- The practice read-back box shows beside the next step when the viewer is about to prepare a handoff (or cannot prepare one), with "Fill an example"; otherwise it is under "Show everything". The receiver sees the real read-back form instead.
- Blocked case (suggested: MOBL-53): a description, a due date later than 2026-12-31 or a missing due date, the component `legacy-billing`, and a prepared handoff to the receiver. The binding decisions are the two sample decisions above.
- Clear case (suggested: TD-1 or MOBL-58): the item meets both decisions (a due date of 2026-12-15), the receiver has given a read-back after the last change to the item, and the item is assigned to the receiver (or the sender, or no one). The validator refuses an item assigned to a third person even when the handoff is Clear.
- Rovo must be enabled on the site for scene 5 (b); pick the development or production agent by its icon in the agent picker, and start a new chat. Do not depend on Rovo for the main story: a judge's site may not have it.
- A Jira admin must have added the Handoff Guard validator to a workflow transition for scene 4.

Rules for the cut: no real customer data, only the demo site. No measurement figure is shown on screen: a percentage in a video is read as accuracy whatever the narration says (council review, 2026-11-14). The figures stay in the README, called "agreement with labels", with the samples stated as synthetic and the labels as model-written.

Out of the video, in the product and the docs only: Bounce autopsy, proposals from "Find decisions with Rovo", Leaving the project, the I-PASS labels, Activity, counts, export and delete, adding a decision by hand, Replay. Scene 3 needs two accounts: it is the only place a judge sees Clear.
