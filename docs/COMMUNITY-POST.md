**Title:** Looking for a few Jira users to try a handoff app (Codegeist 2026 entry)

Hi all,

I built a small Forge app for Jira as my entry to the Codegeist 2026 hackathon, and I am looking for three to five people who reassign work in Jira to try it and tell me where it is confusing. I am the author; this is not a commercial listing, and please do not leave ratings or reviews for it.

**What it does, in one line:** when a work item is handed to someone else, plain rules check what is missing and which recorded team decisions bind it, and the receiver restates each decision before taking the work.

**What I am asking:** about 20 minutes on a test Jira site where you are an admin (a free site is fine; please not your work site).

Good to know before you start:

- The install screen asks for four permissions (Jira work item data, Teamwork Graph work items and users, app storage) and nothing for Confluence. The app makes no calls outside Atlassian and you can uninstall it afterwards.
- With one account the handoff ends Blocked with "Sender and receiver are the same person". That is the rule working, not a fault. A second user as receiver can reach Clear.
